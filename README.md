# TPT-MOD-Leaf-s-Beautiful-World
>No reposting this mod, edited or vanilla without permission &lt; A LUA mod for the powder toy, AI was used for research and bug clearing. Adds many elements, like Sulfur, Methane, Alcohol, Crude Oil, Nitric and Sulphuric acid, and mercury fulminate! Aswell as reactions, like crude oil distilling, the acids creations, and alcohol "fermentation" 
--
Reactions
CRDE 200C+ 1% per tick to: 40% chance of oil, 30% chance of NPTH, 25% chance of SALT, 15% chance of GLOW
NPTH when catalyzed with PTNM may create: 70% chance of DESL, 20% chance of BCOL, and a 10% chance of creating DSTW.
YEST may ferment SUGR into ALCH if in presence of OXYG, when fermented SUGR becomes ALCH and OXYG becomes MTHN, YEST is unaffected.
ALCH will damage STKM
NITA can be created when WTRV+NTRO combine at temperatures at 150 Celsius or more. Creates HYGN Aswell.
Similarly, SFAC can be created with SULF and WTRV at any temperature.
Acid Behavior for SFAC and NTRO. Things it wont dissolve (includes walls): ACID, WATR, DSTW, GLAS, DMND, MERC, WTRV, SLTW, GOLD, SFAC, NITA, NITR, SULF, NTRO, MFLM.
SFAC+NITA=NITR
MERC+NITA=MFLM+NTRO
--
Elements: SULF Sulfur, MFLM Mercury Fulminate, MTHN Methane, CRDE Crude Oil, NPTH Naptha, SUGR Sugar, ALCH Alcohol, NTRO Nitrogen, SFAC Sulfuric Acid, NITA Nitric Acid, HELM Helium.
